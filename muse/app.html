    What is Muse?

    Muse is a small instrument you play by drawing. Press and drag anywhere on the canvas: the height of your cursor or finger picks one of 16 notes, and the further right you go, the louder it plays. Hold still and the note repeats on the beat.

    There are thirteen sounds to choose from: eight sampled ones (Bass, Shimmer, Pad, Tone, Breath, Warm, Pluck and Lead), a drum kit with a different drum on every row, and synthesized Piano, Marimba, Strings and Synth. Pick one with the buttons at the top, the 1 to 0 keys, or [ and ] to step through them all. It works with a mouse, a pen, or several fingers at once on a phone or tablet.

    Play together

    Pick a room, or name a new one, and share the link. Everyone in the same room sees each other's cursors and hears each other's notes live, locked to one shared beat. No account or install needed.
