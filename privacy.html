Privacy

last updated November 9, 2026

This site (joshwein.xyz, including the blog at /blog) is a personal site. I don't sell your data, and I don't ask for an account. Some third-party services it uses do set cookies, and this page lists them.

Ads (Google AdSense)

Blog posts may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and to other sites on the internet. You can turn off personalized ads in Google's Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

The site uses Google Analytics to count page views and see roughly how people find it. It collects things like the pages you visit, your browser, and your approximate location, and it sets cookies to do so. See Google's privacy policy.

Blog comments (Disqus)

Comments on blog posts are run by Disqus, which loads on post pages and may set its own cookies. Anything you post there falls under Disqus's privacy policy.

Contact form (Formspree)

The contact page sends your message, and your email if you give one, to me through Formspree. I use it only to read and reply to your message.

Muse

The Muse music app uses Firebase anonymous sign-in to sync multiplayer rooms. It doesn't ask for your name or email.

Questions

If you have a question or want something removed, reach me through the contact page.
